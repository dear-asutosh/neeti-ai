This document contains targeted questions and answers designed for pitch sessions, jury evaluations, and high-stakes meetings.

## 💸 Section 1: Investor & Business Queries

## 1. What is your unit economics model for Neeti AI, and what is the estimated cost per official?

Answer: We operate on a SaaS (Software as a Service) per-seat model. Our unit economics are highly favorable because our underlying Inference Engine (Groq) is optimized for high-speed, low-cost token usage. We estimate the cost per official per month to be competitive with standard cloud office suites.

## 2. What is your deployment timeline for a new administrative department or ward?

Answer: We pride ourselves on a **2-week implementation cycle**. This includes initial workspace setup, domain-restricted authentication configuration, and training for the core administrative staff. Our platform architecture allows for instant global deployment.

## 3. Who is your first paying customer?

Answer: We are currently in advanced pilot discussions with a Regional Municipal Corporation and are targeting District Collectorates in the next phase. Our first paying customer is expected to be a forward-thinking MLA office looking to modernize their constituency management.

## 4. What is your "Moat" or competitive advantage against big tech entering the space?

Answer: Our moat is **Domain Specialization and Bharat-Focus**. While generic LLMs exist, Neeti AI is the "last-mile" integration layer specifically tuned for Indian administrative workflows and regional languages (12+ supported).

## 🏛️ Section 2: Government & Administrative Queries

## 5. Can Neeti AI handle our specific department-level workflows, like Rural Development?

Answer: Yes. The system is modular, so we can activate or deactivate widgets based on your specific requirements. We have a live demo ready that showcases how the platform can be customized for different departments.

## 6. What technical evidence can you provide for data security and sovereignty in government work?

Answer: We use a **Direct-to-Cloud architecture with 256-bit HTTPS encryption**. Data is stored in isolated Firestore buckets. Most importantly, we have Zero-Retention policies on our inference engines (Groq), meaning your deliberations are never used to train public models.

## 7. Can we deploy this in our specific ward or district immediately, and how?

Answer: Yes. Our implementation is designed for rapid rollout. We can have your specific ward onboarded, your historical data imported, and your staff trained within a **2-week window**.

## 8. How do we ensure that officials with minimal tech experience can use this effectively?

Answer: The UI is designed with a **"People-First" philosophy**. If an official knows how to use a standard messaging app, they can use Neeti AI. We prioritize natural language interaction via the AI Assistant over complex menus.

## 🌍 Section 3: Diplomacy & Policy Queries

## 9. How exactly does this platform strengthen democratic governance and accountability?

Answer: Neeti AI ensures that **every decision is documented, auditable, and data-driven**. By removing the "fog" of fragmented paperwork, we bring transparency to the administrative process, strengthening the trust between the citizen and the state.

## 10. What is the impact of Neeti AI on regional language inclusivity in public offices?

Answer: Neeti AI breaks the language barrier. By supporting **12+ Indian regional languages**, we ensure that governance is inclusive. A leader can hear a grievance in a native tongue and generate a response or report in Marathi, Hindi, or English.

## 11. How does this align with the national "Digital India" vision for the next decade?

Answer: Neeti AI is the "Intelligence Layer" of Digital India. We aren't just digitizing records; we are making those records **intelligent and actionable**, transitioning from a "Digital Database" to an "Artificial Intelligence Command Center."
